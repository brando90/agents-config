# SBSBZ finance rules and their sources

**Doc link:** <https://github.com/brando90/agents-config/blob/main/skills/sbsbz-financial-officer/references/policy.md>

**TLDR:** Each rule the FO agent applies, with where it comes from.
- **Official:** a current Stanford, ASSU (Associated Students of Stanford University), GSC (Graduate Student Council) or OSE (Office of Student Engagement) page. Quotes were checked against the raw page text on 10-09-2026 unless marked otherwise.
- **Club notes:** meeting notes or guidance the FO relayed. Confirm those before they decide money.

Re-check every academic year.

**Source key**
- KB = GrantEd Help Center article `https://sse.frontkb.com/en/articles/<id>`
- `/206` = <https://www.assu.stanford.edu/206/Graduate-VSO-Grants>
- `/196` = <https://www.assu.stanford.edu/196/ASSU-Funding>
- NSF = OSE non-student framework, <https://ose.stanford.edu/student-orgs/policies/non-student-involvement/non-student-framework>

The old `assu.stanford.edu/finances/...` URLs return 404.

## Paying people
| # | Rule | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | An outside instructor or DJ is paid by a GrantEd **Service Payment** after the service. Documentation: "Email/text agreeing on payment amount, invoice with itemized charges, or event flyer." | Official | KB 8112065 |
| P2 | The payee needs a W-9. GrantEd emails the payee a W-9 request when the FO enters their name and email; resends go through `banking@sse.stanford.edu`. | Official | KB 8112065, 8112129 |
| P3 | Peer-to-peer apps "such as Venmo, Zelle, or Apple Cash are not allowed for any services from people." An organizer who pays an instructor by Venmo should not expect reimbursement. | Official | KB 8112129 |
| P4 | Outside payees get a check, by mail or pickup. Direct deposit requires Axess, so it is for Stanford students only. | Official | KB 2852097, 8755457; ASSU FAQ /211 |
| P5 | "If you have not been approved prior to your payment request, your request will be rejected." | Official | KB 8755457 |
| P6 | Service payments of $1,000 or more need OSE approval; $10,000 or more needs OSE review plus a contract. | Official (research report; the table was not re-quoted) | KB 8112065; OSE contracts page |
| P7 | "DO NOT TRY TO SELF REIMBURSE." | Official | KB 8112065 |
| P8 | Instructors paid by service payment are independent contractors of the ASSU. An instructor employed by Stanford needs pre-approval from their department. | Official | NSF |
| P9 | An **ongoing** coach or instructor needs a Registration Form (the Non-Student Involvement Form in CardinalEngage), a HireRight background check and Core 10 Policies training. "Significant" contact means more than twice a month. | Official | NSF |
| P10 | GSC honoraria cannot pay on-campus scholars: faculty, visiting scholars, postdocs or students. Officers may not be paid for normal club activities. | Official (research report; not re-quoted 10-09-2026) | GSC 2026–27 funding guidelines; KB 3316289 |
| P11 | Pay instructors per class, after the class is taught, attaching the written agreement on the amount. | Club practice | 05-03-2026 FO meeting |
| P12 | Each instructor invoice must be under $200 and submitted separately. | **Not found** in official pages (official thresholds: $1,000, $10,000) | Brando, 09-25-2026; confirm with SSE |

## GSC funding
| # | Rule | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | "The GSC does not provide retroactive funding." | Official | /206 |
| G2 | "Funding requests must be submitted by Sunday, 5 pm" to be voted on at that week's GSC meeting. | Official | /206 |
| G3 | Lead time: ≤$500 needs 3 meetings before the event and no attendance; ≤$1,000 needs 4 meetings; >$1,000 needs 5 meetings and attendance at a GSC meeting. Hence monthly requests under $1,000. | Official | /206 |
| G4 | ASSU applications "must be submitted at least one month before the event date"; requests a week or less ahead may be denied. | Official | /196 (also relayed by FO 09-21-2026) |
| G5 | Every GSC-funded event goes on the GSC Funding Calendar at least 14 days ahead (mandatory). | Official | GrantEd Quick Grant question text; GSC guidelines |
| G6 | "At least 30% graduate versus undergraduate participation." | Official | /206 |
| G7 | GSC wants an attendee list; a photo is not enough. | Club notes (reviewer feedback relayed by FO, 09-23-2026) | FO chat |
| G8 | Unspent allocations disappear at fiscal-year end. Official pages say only that the fiscal year turns over June 30, with no extensions past it. | Observed in the statement (06-30-2025 "GSC takebacks") | VSO statement export |
| G9 | Food: up to $20 per student per meal, snacks up to $8; a quote is required above $500; tips at most 20%. | Official (research report) | KB 3316353, 2851777 |

## Money coming in
| # | Rule | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Do not collect through Venmo/Zelle/PayPal; cash donations and passing the hat are not allowed. | Official (research report) | OSE ticketing and fundraising pages |
| M2 | Class or instruction fees go through Eventbrite, not ASSU ePay. ePay covers sponsorships, sales, general revenue and dues, and accepts payments from non-affiliates. | Official (research report) | KB 8681281; ASSU /210 |
| M3 | Groups "may charge a reasonable fee to offset the cost of participants who do not pay student fees." | Official (research report) | NSF |

## Not found on official pages (10-09-2026)
- A reimbursement deadline after purchase.
- A "Compensation" account. The club's own statement does show a `Compensation - VSO` line used for weekly classes in 01-2026, so match whichever account the approved funding line uses.
- A general June 30 takeback rule.
- Sales tax on reimbursements.
