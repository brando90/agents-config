## What the slides add to the skill

1. **Sources for each lesson.** The recording names papers aloud, sometimes garbled by automatic captions ("Lillian Wang", "Goodart's law"). The slides carry the paper links and screenshots; the skill's references now cite them.
2. **Lessons skipped or rushed in the recording.** These are the Uber forecasting data story; "No Task Is Beneath You"; "Invest in Engineering Skills"; "Don't Get Nerdsniped by Math"; the three career lessons "How to Choose Projects", "Deciding Whether to Persevere or Pivot" and "Much of the Field Has Been Frequently Wrong"; and the personal lessons "Reward Hacking Is Cheating Y…", "Pride is Costly" and most of "You Are More Capable Than You Believe". Most are title-only slides or screenshots, so their argument is not recoverable from the deck; the rows below give only what the slides show.
3. **Exact wording of the "Other Comments" slide (53)**, which the recording covers in about two minutes: "Iteration velocity is the best predictor of success (models training; researchers experimenting). Be paranoid about everything. If your result is too good to be true, it probably isn't. Consistency is your best debugger. Take one risk at a time. Invest in Engineering Skills. Product & research live in tension. Don't Get Nerdsniped by Math."
