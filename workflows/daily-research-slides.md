Keep a link to that archive; do not erase the history of corrected claims.
- A run with no material change leaves content and user notifications quiet. A missing result is not progress. Record a blocking edit/access failure in the existing checkpoint and notify only when action is needed; do not claim a deck was refreshed if the read-back failed.

## Evidence on the slide and in its notes

Read the latest canonical results and underlying receipts before updating claims. Distinguish candidate, reference-accepted, evaluated, packaged, handed-off and recipient-acknowledged counts. A passed review or prepared archive is not delivery; missing recipient acknowledgement does not erase a verified handoff. Preserve exact task IDs and frozen versions in the linked provenance, particularly when accepted successors differ from historical evaluated tasks.

Show the named metric, scale, full task/panel denominator, missing outcomes, agent/model and compared condition wherever needed to interpret the headline. Keep smooth and harsh scoring distinct; specify the actual aggregation and harsh success rule in notes. A prospective target such as harsh performance below 0.60 is user-chosen guidance, not a measured outcome or a replacement for reference validity. Do not select only failures, suppress valid successes, silently change protocols or treat stricter scoring as evidence of formal source verification.

Selection effects, incomplete reviews, uncertainty that changes the conclusion, judge limitations and incomparable versions belong visibly beside the claim. Put full calculations, confidence intervals, resampling unit, provenance, exact model/harness/budget, cost and reproducibility links in notes or an appendix, following [statistics reporting](statistics-reporting.md). Comments may supplement those details but must not be their only durable home. Use existing evidence only; a scheduled slide refresh does not authorize new model calls or experiments.
